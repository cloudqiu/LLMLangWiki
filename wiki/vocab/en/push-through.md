# push through

**释义**：phr. v. ①硬推过去、挤过去——push through the crowd ②（使）强行通过（法案 / 决议 / 计划）——push a bill through (parliament) ③挺过去、熬过（困难 / 病痛）——push through the pain ｜ to force a way through; to get (a measure, deal, plan) accepted despite opposition; to endure and survive (difficulty, pain)

**例句**
- _We pushed through the crowd to reach the stage._ 我们挤过人群来到台前。
- _The government pushed the bill through parliament._ 政府强行让法案在议会获得通过。
- _She pushed through the pain and finished the marathon._ 她忍痛坚持，跑完了马拉松。

**词源 / 构词**
- **拆解**：**push**（v.）+ **through**（副词 / 介词「穿过」）
- **演变**：**push**（v.）首见 **约 1300**，作 **pushen**，义「shove or move onward by thrusting（推、顶动）」（据 etymonline）；← 古法 **poulser**（今法 pousser）← 拉丁 **pulsare**「to beat, strike, push（打、击、推）」，是 **pellere**（过去分词 **pulsus**「to push, drive, beat」）的**反复态（frequentative）**，Watkins 溯至 PIE 词根 **\*pel- (5)**「to thrust, strike, drive（推、刺、驱）」。义项扩展：**1570s** 及物「urge, incite, press（催促、鼓动、催逼）」；**1714**「以持续努力推动 / 促成（某事）」；**1718** 不及物「make one's way with force and persistence（以力与坚持开出路）」；**1937**「将近（某年龄）」。相关成语：push (sb) around（**1923**，欺负 / 摆布）、push one's luck（**1754**）、push the envelope（**late 1980s**）；n. push 1560s 起（1590s「a vigorous attempt」；**1855**「persevering enterprise」）；when push comes to shove（**by 1936**）。**注意**：etymonline **未单列 push through 短语条**——短语本身首见年代**未提供（待验证）**；其「使（法案）通过」义对应 push 的 **1714**「促成 / 推进」义
- **同族 / 派生**：**push** (n./v.)、**pushing**、**pushy**、**pushover**、**pushback**、**push chair**；近义短语 [[carve-out]]（开辟出）、[[cling-to]]（紧抓不放），可作语义场对照
- **记忆钩**：**push（推）+ through（穿） = 一路推着穿过去**。三层引申：物理的「挤过人群」→ 抽象的「顶着反对把法案推过（关卡）」→ 承受力上的「带着痛把它推完（熬过去）」

**同义 / 搭配 / 易混**
- 搭配：push through the crowd / the door；push a bill / a law / a motion / a deal / a plan through (parliament / Congress)；push through the pain / the setback / the opposition
- 同义：使通过——force through、railroad through、steamroll、drive through；挤过——squeeze through、shove through、thread one's way；挺过去——ride out、weather、persevere、see through
- 易混 / 辨析：**push through vs push for**——push through＝**使（议案）获通过（结果 / 完成）**，push for＝**力争、呼吁（过程 / 诉求）**；**push through vs [[carve-out]]**——push through 侧重**克服阻力推动使通过**，carve out 侧重凭努力**开辟出（一片天地）**
